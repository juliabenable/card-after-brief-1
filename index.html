Brief first, card to accept

Today a creator without a card hits the card ask before they can open a single brief: a popup when they land on Campaigns, then "Add a card to accept" where "View Details" used to be. The change: let everyone read the brief, and ask for the card only when they tap Accept & Commit. Tap any phone to see it full size. High-fidelity frames: Figma, "Credit Card Requirement · Brief first (Nov 2)".

The direction Decided · Julia · Nov 2

  • Every creator can open and read the full brief, with or without a card.
  • A card is required to accept. Accept & Commit opens the card sheet, and saving the card accepts in the same tap.
  • Onboarding stays as it is: the card is still the last step, with no skip.
  • The "Add a card to join campaigns" drawer opens on arrival only for creators with no card and no open invite. Creators with an invite go straight to the brief.
  • Ops can waive the card from admin, three ways: this campaign only, this campaign and every future one or forever. The creator sees "We waived the card requirement" in the app.
  • Unchanged: everyone in a gifted campaign still has a card on file (Tony, Oct 16). Local and gift-card campaigns stay exempt.
